Executive Summary
The RSV Infotech / Netopsys Document Management System currently supports Purchase Orders, Quotations, Tax Invoices, Delivery Orders, and Goods Receipt Notes across three company entities. This proposal outlines 15 targeted enhancements across four structured phases over an estimated 10 months. The enhancements span new operational modules (Credit Notes, Purchase Requisitions, Expense Claims), financial intelligence tools (Invoice Aging, Partial Payments), workflow automation, and platform-wide UX improvements. Each phase is designed to deliver standalone business value so that work may be paused or reprioritised between phases without disrupting the live system.

Credit Notes
Issue a credit note linked to an existing Tax Invoice — for goods returns, overbilling corrections, or goodwill credits. Credit notes have their own running number (CN-XXXXXX), PDF generation, and email delivery. Full audit trail against the source invoice.

Partial Payments on Invoices
Record multiple payment instalments against a single invoice — each with a date, amount, and reference. Track outstanding balance automatically and mark the invoice as fully paid when settled. Supports both cash and bank transfer references.

A dedicated report page showing all outstanding invoices grouped into aging buckets: 0–30 days, 31–60 days, 61–90 days, and 90+ days overdue. Helps the finance team identify and chase overdue payments. Exportable to PDF and Excel.

Invoices

Automatically flag quotations approaching or past their 30-day validity window. Show a warning badge on the list and detail pages. Optionally send an automated email reminder to the customer before the quotation expires.

09
Document Linking & Conversion
Convert a confirmed Quotation directly into a Tax Invoice with one click, carrying over all line items. Link POs to their GRNs and Delivery Orders. Each document shows a visual "document trail" (QT → INV, PO → GRN → DO) for full traceability.
Enhancement

10
Purchase Requisitions (PR)
Internal approval workflow: staff raise a Purchase Requisition; an admin reviews and approves or rejects it; approved PRs can be converted into a Purchase Order in one click. Provides spending control and a procurement audit trail before any external PO is issued.
New Module
Linked to: PO
High
11
Recurring Invoices
Schedule an invoice to auto-generate on a monthly or quarterly cadence — useful for retainer-based or subscription contracts. The system creates a draft invoice automatically on the due date, which staff review and confirm before sending to the customer.
Enhancement
Invoices
Medium
12
Financial-Year Running Numbers
Option to reset document counters at the start of the financial year (April for Singapore / India) rather than monthly. Configurable per company. Supports formats like INV-FY2627-0001. No impact on existing historical documents.

Risks & Mitigations
Risk Likelihood Mitigation
Scope creep between phases Medium Each phase is independently deliverable. New requests are queued to the next phase rather than inserted mid-sprint.
Data migration complexity (Vendor/Customer directories) Low Directories are additive — existing documents are unaffected. Historical records are not required to reference directory entries.
User adoption of new modules (PR, Expense Claims) Medium Each new module includes a short user guide and an admin walk-through session before go-live.
Timeline slippage on high-effort items Low–Medium High-effort features (Credit Notes, Document Linking, PR) are scoped with buffer weeks. Phase end-dates are target, not hard deadlines.
Assumptions & Dependencies
  • The existing system infrastructure (PostgreSQL database, Express API, React frontend) remains unchanged throughout all phases.
  • Business requirements for each phase will be confirmed and signed off by stakeholders at least two weeks before development begins.
  • SMTP email configuration remains active for notification features (Quotation Expiry Alerts, Recurring Invoices).
  • File storage (for Expense Claim receipts) will use the existing cloud object storage already provisioned for the platform.
  • User testing and sign-off for each phase will be completed within one week of feature delivery before the next phase begins.
